How a bag gets to you

Green coffee comes through Genuine Origin, Red Fox Coffee Merchants and Royal Coffee NYC. Everything after that happens here.

  1. Monday

    Roasting day

    Everything goes through the drum in small batches. Every order is roasted the Monday before it leaves, not pulled off a shelf.

  2. Tuesday

    Orders ship

    Out the door by USPS Priority 2-Day Express or UPS Ground Advantage, with tracking, so you can follow the bag.

  3. Tuesday

    Or collect it

    Pickup at the roastery between 9 and 5. If that window does not work, say so and it gets worked out.

  4. Wednesday

    Local delivery

    Free, within four miles of the roastery.

Questions

Where does the coffee come from?

Green coffee is sourced through three importers — Genuine Origin, Red Fox Coffee Merchants and Royal Coffee NYC — and roasted here in Gainesville.

I ordered coffee. When will it arrive?

Roasting happens on Mondays and orders ship on Tuesdays, by USPS Priority 2-Day Express or UPS Ground Advantage. Tracking follows by email.

Can I pick my order up instead?

Yes — at the roastery on Tuesdays between 9 and 5. If you cannot make that window, get in touch and it will be worked out.

Do you deliver locally?

Free local delivery on Wednesdays, anywhere within four miles of the roastery.

I want to serve your coffee at my restaurant, cafe or bar.

Wholesale is open. Email afternoonroasting@gmail.com and the conversation starts there.
